Root cause — strategic view

Operating churn grouped by the response it requires.

Stakeholder / business changes

14 events · 23.2%
$361,708

The majority of this is EBY. Customer-side leadership changes, business pivots, and moves to other selling models.

The solve: We can solve for this, but it cannot be reactive. This is preventable with narrower focuses and smaller books.

PAs: Increased focus on getting accounts into the "winning zone": healthy margins, optimized attach rate, conversion rate, and other risk factors. This means saying "not now" to other asks from the business. Churn is top priority.

AMs: We're hiring more AMs to tackle this. It's already improving as we hire. Once staffed, we'll cover 95% of our revenue with book sizes that allow that level of proactivity.

Key accounts: EBY ($286K), PROJECT SOCIAL T ($20K), The Western Company ($14K), Incase.com ($11K), YES HOMO ($11K).

Pricing & value concerns

15 events · 22.0%
$342,074

Deals priced too high, unclear value story, or proactive corrections to keep economics aligned. Harmonize belongs here because it was high-margin, not negative-margin.

The solve: Spencer Evans and Haden rolled out a new pricing table to better calculate return rate and better handle multi-product deals. We rolled out stricter requirements around high-margin deals.

Key accounts: Formuland ($143K), Impact Dog Crates ($60K), Harmonize Beauty ($57K), forever french baby ($47K), AZVA ($8K).

Coverage / product concerns

20 events · 19.9%
$309,849

Product fit, coverage-model objections, conversion concerns, warranty design, and cases where merchants wanted to own the economics.

The solve: Seth has done great work on revamped SE playbooks to better catch and handle these before go-live. Tyler Welch and I put together a sales training to drive the points home for AEs, and we'll have a monthly cadence to review this at the beginning of each month.

Key accounts: Proline Range Hoods ($105K), Bullstrap ($67K), Pillow Cube ($42K), Ultimate Sack ($26K), Cadence ($20K).

Product / technical

13 events · 19.3%
$300,423

Integration failures, currency support, label generation, refund errors, and edge-case workflows. Customers liked the core product but specific functionality broke trust.

The solve: Creating an Integrations Solution Architect role to test, validate, and troubleshoot integrations issues during the SMB and lower Growth onboarding processes. The role will also collaborate with the Returns Integration team to ensure builds have the right capabilities for merchants.

Increased focus on making the product more usable for merchants. Jesse and Jordan have shipped usability improvements and will continue to do so.

Key accounts: Chirp ($110K), TULIO Fashion ($70K), Geode Swimwear ($61K), Precious Moments ($32K), CULT CREW ($12K).

Contract / commercial issues

The solve: PAs focusing on getting accounts into the "winning zone".
